🔊 Pronunciation Practice ▼

Ayo latihan mengeja kata (phonics)! Tirukan suara yang kamu dengar:

  • swimming dieja: SWI - MMING
  • reading dieja: REA - DING
  • singing dieja: SI - NG - ING
💬 Let's Talk! (Percakapan) ▼

Ajak temanmu praktik berbicara seperti Joshua & Aisyah:

Joshua:
"Do you like swimming?"
(Apakah kamu suka berenang?)
Aisyah:
"Yes, I like swimming."
(Ya, aku suka berenang.)
Joshua:
"Does Made like football?"
(Apakah Made suka main bola?)
Aisyah:
"No, he likes riding a bike."
(Tidak, dia suka bersepeda.)
