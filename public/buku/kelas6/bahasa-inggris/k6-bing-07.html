📖 Bagian 2: Grammar & Reading ▼

📝 Grammar Focus

Future Simple "will": rencana masa depan, prediksi, keputusan spontan. Rumus: Subject + will + base verb. Negative: will not (won't). Question: Will you...? Contoh: I will go. She will visit. They won't come. Will we meet?

I will go to Bromo next month.
(Aku akan ke Bromo bulan depan.)
She won't forget her homework.
(Dia tidak lupa PR.)
Will they visit us tomorrow?
(Mereka akan ke sini besok?)
