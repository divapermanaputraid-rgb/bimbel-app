📖 Bagian 2: Grammar & Reading ▼

📝 Grammar Focus

Feelings = perasaan. Past: feel → felt. How did you feel? = Bagaimana perasaanmu? Jawab dengan "I felt [adjective]." Contoh: I felt happy. She felt nervous. They felt excited.

I felt happy when I won.
(Aku senang waktu menang.)
She felt nervous before the test.
(Dia gugup sebelum ujian.)
They felt excited about the trip.
(Mereka bersemangat soal trip.)

📖 Let's Read!

Yesterday was an emotional day. Cici won the spelling competition. She felt happy and proud. Her friend Made lost the football match. He felt sad and a little angry. But then Cici cheered him up. "Don't worry! You'll do better next time." Made felt better and thanked her. In the end, both felt grateful for their friendship.
