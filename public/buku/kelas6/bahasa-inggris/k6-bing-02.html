📖 Bagian 2: Grammar & Reading ▼

📝 Grammar Focus

Recount text = cerita pengalaman masa lalu. Struktur: Orientation (siapa, kapan, di mana) → Events (urut waktu) → Re-orientation (kesan). Gunakan time connectives: First, Then, After that, Finally.

First, we visited the museum.
(Pertama, kita kunjungi museum.)
Then, we ate traditional food.
(Kemudian, kita makan makanan tradisional.)
Finally, we went home happily.
(Akhirnya, kita pulang dengan senang.)

📖 Let's Read!

Last holiday, Cici went to Yogyakarta with her family. First, they visited Borobudur Temple early in the morning. The sunrise was beautiful! Then, they walked around Malioboro Street and ate gudeg for lunch. After that, they bought batik shirts as souvenirs. Finally, they took a train back home. It was an amazing experience!
