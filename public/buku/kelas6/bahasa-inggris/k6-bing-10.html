📖 Bagian 2: Grammar & Reading ▼

📝 Grammar Focus

Jobs = pekerjaan. Want to be = ingin menjadi. Contoh: I want to be a pilot. She wants to be a doctor (dia mau jadi dokter — tambah s untuk he/she). Why? Because... = Karena... Reason: I like flying. / I want to help people.

I want to be a teacher.
(Aku mau jadi guru.)
She wants to be an astronaut.
(Dia mau jadi astronot.)
Why? Because I like helping people.
(Karena aku suka bantu orang.)
