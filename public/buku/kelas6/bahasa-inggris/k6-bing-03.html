📖 Bagian 2: Grammar & Reading ▼

📝 Grammar Focus

Past Simple "be": was (I/he/she/it) — were (you/we/they). Contoh: I was in Bali. They were happy. Negative: was not (wasn't) / were not (weren't). Question: Was she...? / Were they...?

I was at the beach yesterday.
(Aku di pantai kemarin.)
They were not at home.
(Mereka tidak di rumah.)
Was the water cold?
(Apakah airnya dingin?)

📖 Let's Read!
