📖 Bagian 2: Grammar & Reading ▼

📝 Grammar Focus

Future time adverbials = penanda waktu masa depan. Posisi: akhir kalimat atau awal. Contoh: I will go tomorrow. Next week, she will visit. In 3 days, we will leave. Soon = segera (tidak spesifik).

I will go tomorrow.
(Aku akan pergi besok.)
Next week, she will start school.
(Minggu depan, dia mulai sekolah.)
We will meet in two days.
(Kita akan ketemu dalam 2 hari.)
